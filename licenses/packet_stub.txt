packet.dll in the Windows client
--------------------------------

stand-in built from client/packaging/packet_stub.c in the Neutrino

What it is: easytier-core.exe imports eleven functions from Packet.dll
through the pnet crate, and Windows does not start it without a Packet.dll
to load. The stand-in exports those eleven functions, and each answers
failure or nothing: the client never opens a capture adapter. No code of
Npcap is in it, and Npcap is neither carried nor needed.
